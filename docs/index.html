01 / RESEARCH

Connecting physical insight
with computational possibility.

My research sits at the intersection of computational mechanics, scientific machine learning and resilient infrastructure. I develop physics-informed and data-driven methods that retain engineering meaning while reducing the cost of repeated numerical analysis.

At NTU, I lead the research theme AI for Structures Against Multi-Hazards as part of the Protective Technology Research Centre core team. My work spans full-field thermal and blast prediction, experimental structural assessment, and material-efficient concrete systems.
