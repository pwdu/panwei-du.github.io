Research profile

I am a civil and structural engineering researcher working at the intersection of computational mechanics, artificial intelligence and resilient infrastructure. My research develops physics-informed and data-driven AI methods, including neural operators, surrogate modelling and transfer learning, for rapid assessment of structural and infrastructure response under fire and blast hazards.

Research interests: scientific machine learning for civil engineering; structural fire and protective engineering; concrete materials and structures at elevated temperatures; sustainable concrete and composite structural systems.
